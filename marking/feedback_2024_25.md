Then, the bulk of the submissions indicated a relatively good understanding of the concepts introduced during the lectures. Of course, there was quite some variation, but all these 'middle' range submissions, were primarily based on what was discussed during the class and illustrated a good undersstanding. Lastly, there were a handful of projects that, regrettably, did not meet the expectations of the assignment and, therefore, no pass mark was given. All in all, I am happy with the skills the group developed during the past term.
