# Urban Analytics and City Science

Thank you all for your hard work during Teaching Block 1. I am aware that for a lot of you there was a steep learning curve, but I hope you enjoyed coursework. 

I was very happy to see a wide range of topics and datasets used for the projects. In general, the results were rather polarised this year. On the one hand, there were a handful of exceptionally good projects at the very top of the marking scale. They were innovative, rigorous and sophisticated. All these submissions employed tools and methods beyond what was taught during the unit in order to answer their research questions. They critically discussed the results and offered meaningful conclusions.

On the other hand, there were quite a lot of submissions that achieved a pass mark. They indicated different degrees of understanding and engagement with the unit's content. All these submissions were based only on what was discussed during the class.

Quite a few submission presented results without properly discussing them and, therefore, were downmarked accordingly. Also, there were a few projects without appropriate referencing. This is something that you will need to sort out sooner rather than later as it is an integral past of the MSc. 

All in all, I am happy with the skills the group developed during the past term.

Prof Emmanouil Tranos
